Rental Policy

Self Drive Terms & Conditions

Please read these rules carefully before taking the vehicle delivery.

General Terms

  • The car is provided on a self-drive basis. No driver will be provided.
  • The renter must carry a valid Driving License and a Government ID Proof.
  • The renter is fully responsible for the vehicle during the rental period.
  • Any damage, accident, traffic challan, or loss will be borne by the renter.
  • Smoking, alcohol consumption, and driving under the influence are strictly prohibited.
  • The vehicle must be returned on time and in the same condition.
  • Late return charges will be applicable as per hourly standard rates.
  • Fuel Policy: Return the vehicle with the same fuel level as provided at delivery.
  • Security Deposit will be refunded after full vehicle inspection upon return.

Required Documents

  • Original & Valid Driving License (DL)
  • Government Photo ID Proof
  • Address Proof
